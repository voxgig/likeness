likeness list [<selector>...] [--limit N] [--raw] [--sort KEY]

notes matching a selector, across the selected connections

  <selector>...  the selector's terms, joined with spaces
  --limit N      at most N notes, 1 to 999999999
  --raw          carry each source's own record
  --sort KEY     order the notes by KEY

global flags: --config PATH, --deterministic, --instance REF..., --json, --passphrase-env VAR, --source API..., --strict, --vault-key NAME
